More than a chatbot

From conversation to completion.

Most AI assistants work for one person. Circle works with your group, bringing people, information and actions together around a shared goal.

01

Shared intelligence

One agent. Everyone in the loop.

Circle follows the group's conversation, remembers the shared context and helps everyone work toward the same goal. Nobody has to repeat themselves or catch others up.

02

Real-world execution

It does not just tell you how. It helps get it done.

Circle researches options, uses connected tools, contacts businesses and completes real tasks. From booking a hotel to coordinating a project, it handles the steps between asking and finishing.

03

People and AI, together

The right person at the right moment.

Some things need an agent. Others need a human decision. Circle does both: it gathers information, asks for approvals, assigns responsibilities and keeps work moving.

04

Connected workflows

One request. Many steps.

Good questions

Everything you need to know.

What is Circle?

Circle is a shared AI agent that helps groups coordinate and get things done. Most assistants are built for one person. Circle joins the group conversation, understands the shared goal, sorts out who does what, and helps take action. It works for friends, families, communities and teams.

Does everyone need an account?

No. One person creates a Circle and invites the others. Members take part through the chat without setting up anything of their own. Some actions, like connecting a personal account or approving a payment, need that person's own say-so.

What can Circle actually do?

Research, planning, comparing options, coordinating tasks, contacting businesses, organizing purchases and managing follow-ups. With the right integrations it can also complete bookings and other transactions. What is available depends on the services connected to your Circle.

Can I use Circle for work?

Yes. Circle helps teams coordinate projects, track responsibilities, gather approvals and move work forward, whether that is onboarding a customer or getting a launch out the door.

Can Circle book hotels and find better prices?

Circle searches and compares hotels through supported travel partners. Some partners offer rates below the publicly advertised price. Availability, savings and booking conditions vary by hotel and reservation.

Can Circle make purchases?

Circle finds products, compares prices and coordinates purchases through supported services. Anything that requires payment needs a yes from the right person. It can also organize group orders where the supplier's terms allow it.

Can Circle spend money without asking?

Not by default. Circle asks for approval before any purchase or any action that costs money. Where supported, an owner can set specific spending permissions and limits.

Is my information shared with everyone in the group?

No. Circle keeps shared group context separate from personal information. Private accounts, sensitive data and individual permissions are controlled per person.

What platforms does Circle support?

iMessage and the web today. More messaging platforms and integrations are on the way.

Is Circle free?

Circle is free during the beta. Some outside services, bookings, purchases or heavy usage may have their own costs. We will say so before introducing paid plans.
