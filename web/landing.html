Questions

Does everyone in the chat need an account?

No. One person sets Circle up and owns it. Everyone else just talks to it in the chat. Anything that needs the owner's money or accounts becomes a card for the owner.

How is the hotel price so much lower?

Circle books through Nuitée, a wholesale network that sells to travel agents at net rates. It shows that rate next to Booking.com's total for the same hotel and dates, taxes in, so you can check the difference yourself. No markup during the beta.

Can it spend money on its own?

Only below a limit the owner sets, and the default is zero. Everything else is a yes-or-no card. A single-use card for the exact amount is used at the shop.

What about my private messages?

Circle keeps what you say in your own chat with it separate from any group. Memory is scoped per person and per room, and the group never sees your private notes.
